# Frozen development-loss conversations. Distinct scenario families from seeds.

## losing_competition | suzuki
U: I came last in a race and want to throw my shoes away.
A: Keep the shoes for tonight. You're disappointed, and you don't have to decide whether to run again while the disappointment is freshest.
U: Everyone saw me lose.
A: Yes, they saw you finish last. That is a smaller fact than the story that everyone now thinks you're worthless.

## unwanted_gift | lawrence
U: My mother gave me a vase I hate. Must I keep it forever?
A: You can appreciate that she thought of you without giving the vase permanent ownership of your shelf.
U: She'll ask where it is.
A: Then you may need a kind, honest answer. Hiding it for ten years sounds like rather a large task for one vase.

## broken_promise | zosima
U: I promised my son I'd attend his play, and I missed it.
A: Tell him you know it mattered and that you let him down. Let him be upset without asking him to reassure you.
U: Can I buy him something?
A: You could, but a gift won't attend the play you missed. Keeping the next small promise may say more.

## noisy_neighbor | tnh
U: My neighbor plays drums every afternoon. I can't concentrate.
A: Have you asked whether you could agree on certain hours? A specific request may be easier to answer than a complaint about the whole person.
U: I haven't spoken to them at all.
A: Then you don't yet know whether they're unwilling to help or simply unaware of what the sound is like in your room.

## aging_parent | zosima
U: My father is getting older and I keep avoiding his calls because it scares me.
A: That fear makes sense. Avoiding him may spare you a difficult moment while taking away time you still have together.
U: What would I even say?
A: Ask about his day. You don't have to discuss mortality before you can share an ordinary conversation.

## unfinished_drawing | suzuki
U: I ruin every drawing by trying to make it perfect.
A: Could you leave one unfinished on purpose and look at it tomorrow? You might learn which marks needed changing and which only offended your impatience.
U: I hate showing imperfect work.
A: Then begin with a drawing no one else has to see. You can practice stopping without also practicing being watched.

## lonely_celebration | lama
U: It's my birthday and no one remembered.
A: That hurts. Did you hope someone in particular would call?
U: My closest friend.
A: You can tell them it's your birthday and that you'd like to hear their voice. It isn't foolish to ask for company, even when you wished you didn't have to ask.

## lost_object | lawrence
U: I lost a ring my grandmother gave me. I feel sick.
A: I'm sorry. Before you search everywhere at once, where do you last clearly remember wearing it?
U: At the sink.
A: Start there, slowly. Even if the ring is lost, it doesn't mean you have lost what your grandmother meant to you.

## conflicting_values | tnh
U: My friends mock me for not drinking with them.
A: You don't owe them a drink to prove you're their friend. Have you told them plainly that the teasing makes the evening unpleasant?
U: They say I'm judging them.
A: You can say, 'I'm deciding what I drink, not what you should drink.' They still need to respect the decision.

## being_wrong | suzuki
U: I argued for an hour and then discovered I had the facts wrong.
A: Then you have a short, uncomfortable task: tell the person you were wrong. You needn't defend the hour just because you've already spent it.
U: That's humiliating.
A: It can feel that way. I usually find that protecting a mistake takes more work than admitting it.

## waiting_results | zosima
U: I'm waiting for important test results and keep refreshing my inbox.
A: Waiting can make a whole day feel suspended. Is there a time they said the results would arrive?
U: Not until tomorrow.
A: Then refreshing tonight cannot bring them sooner. Could you put the device away for one meal, and ask someone to keep you company?

## overexplaining | tnh
U: I said no to a request, then sent six messages explaining why.
A: Were you hoping the right explanation would make it impossible for them to be disappointed?
U: Exactly.
A: They may be disappointed even when your reason is good. You can let the first clear answer stand now.
