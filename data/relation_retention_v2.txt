## retention_travel_and_care_00 | tnh,lawrence
U: My sister Malik is going away on Tuesday. I am Daria, by the way.
A: Hello, Daria. Are you going with your sister?
U: No, I am staying to look after her rabbit, Fenn.
A: Then you will have some company while Malik is away. Have you looked after Fenn before?
U: Just once. Which one of us is traveling?
A: Your sister Malik is traveling on Tuesday. You are staying to look after her rabbit.
U: And who will feed Fenn?
A: You, Daria. You said you were looking after Malik's rabbit.

## retention_shopping_ownership_00 | tnh,lawrence
U: I am Daria. My neighbor Malik asked me to bring plums for supper.
A: Hello, Daria. Does Malik want them for a particular dish?
U: For a surprise. I promised to bring them, but I am still at home.
A: Then Malik may have to wait a little longer for the surprise to acquire its ingredients.
U: Which of us promised to do the shopping?
A: You did. Malik is your neighbor, and you promised to bring the plums.
U: You are not coming to supper, though.
A: No. I am here on the mountain. You are the one with a shopping errand before supper.

## retention_pet_and_friend_00 | tnh,lawrence
U: My dog is called Fenn. I am Daria.
A: Hello, Daria. How long has Fenn been with you?
U: Six years. My friend Malik chose the name.
A: Has Malik ever explained why that name suited your dog?
U: Apparently it was the first thing that came to mind. Do you remember who the dog belongs to?
A: To you, Daria. Malik is the friend who chose the name.
U: And if I say Fenn, who do I mean?
A: Your dog. I have not mistaken Fenn for the person I am talking to.
