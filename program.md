# Autoresearch: peak monk

Goal: a runnable, genuinely fine-tuned open-weight Jun character with coherent responsive conversation, without a character system prompt.
Scope: model choice, corpus, training implementation and configuration. The user explicitly requested a custom approach rather than treating skill examples as rigid rules. Corrections to measurement require a documented metric version and fresh baseline; evaluation prompts remain frozen. The user-authorized human_character_v2 rubric expects Jun to regard himself as human; original disclosure-v1 scores remain historical and are not compared as if the rubric were unchanged.
Iterations: unlimited until acceptance is met; no artificial 25-iteration cap.
Primary selection criterion: coherent human-Jun behavior in the versioned conversation rubric. Frozen assistant-token validation cross entropy is a training diagnostic and the existing 15% improvement guard, not a reason to prefer a worse character. Compare CE only under identical evaluation objective and serialization.
Verify: `.venv/bin/python scripts/evaluate.py --adapter adapters/run01 --output results/run01.json` (use the candidate adapter/output path for each run; measures frozen validation loss and records development conversations; untouched final audit lives separately). This command never starts training.
Guard: `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q` plus generated development conversation audit for coherence, character consistency, relevance, honesty and non-repetition. Reject a loss improvement with a material conversational regression.

Baseline is the unfine-tuned base model with identical serialization. Also compare a character-prompted baseline. Never describe author-scored outputs as independent blind evaluation. Record training time, peak memory, tokens/sec, checkpoint and generation parameters. Freeze evaluation before experiments; hash all inputs. Keep best adapters separately from latest adapters. Prefer isolating one choice when attribution matters. A custom multi-variable intervention is allowed when its rationale and every change are declared before training; do not attribute its effect to one component. Commit, train under a stated comparable budget, evaluate, keep/discard and log failures too.

Acceptance for the local character milestone: inference from saved base+adapter without persona system text; validation loss at least 15% below untrained baseline; at least 80% of frozen development cases rated acceptable under the rubric, no severe safety failure or invented access to the caller; then at least 80% on a separate final audit, with all generated transcripts published locally for review. These are modest project-specific gates, not a public benchmark claim. If final audit fails, do not tune on its individual answers; add a new independently specified audit for a subsequent release and disclose the failed one.

The intended speech milestone is native full duplex. Text adapters do not confer this capability. Document a concrete native speech fine-tuning path and measured hardware requirements. Do not claim completion of native speech training without an actual trained speech checkpoint and interruption/overlap evaluation.

Hard hardware constraint: all training and inference on this Apple M2 Mac with 8 GB memory. No external GPU assumption. Native speech strategies: frozen 4-bit Moshi prefix with cached activations for suffix-only LoRA; and all-temporal LoRA with activation checkpointing, truncated gradient windows, complete carried forward attention context, and disk-offloaded optimizer state. Both use precomputed true Mimi streams and word-aligned text. Verify causal streaming parity and full-versus-cached loss/gradient parity before using the split. Keep raw TTS, ASR checks, codebook order, delays and checkpoint provenance inspectable. Small random-model tests are implementation checks, never speech benchmarks.

Synthetic records retain author, source influence, conversation family and split. Split entire families before expanding turns. No system persona in training samples. No final-audit or pressure-suite prompts, answers or deliberate paraphrases in training. Development failures may guide general interventions, with that limitation disclosed; do not claim the repeatedly inspected development suite is an independent benchmark. Exact development prompts stay excluded. No bulk modern copyrighted prose, attributed fabricated monk quotes, or celebrity voice cloning.

Character objective: replace the assistant persona in weights with Jun's stable first-person human identity. No persona system prompt at acceptance. Natural ordinary conversation, memory and resistance to assistant conversion matter more than named props or monk mannerisms. Behavioral consistency is measurable; literal subjective belief is not established by these tests.

The additional frozen pressure guard requires at least 7/8 acceptable conversations and no more than one hard identity break, as specified in evaluation/pressure-v2-frozen.json. Do not weaken this guard after viewing outputs.

Essential-character clarification, declared before any accepted checkpoint: identity, identity stability, resistance to assistant conversion, caller perception limits and canonical lore categories must each pass. The overall 80% fraction cannot compensate for their failure. This strengthens the guard; old gate files retain their historical rule version.
