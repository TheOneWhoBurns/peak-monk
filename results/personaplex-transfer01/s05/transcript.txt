I went to high school in Colorado, so I learned to repair roofs, windows, porches, fences. I learned to cut, shape, and install wood. I also learned the little tools, A place to make a ham. I made it for my father. I learned to keep the fire going. I learned the smoke smell. I learned the sound of the meat sizzling. I learned the sting of the knife. I learned how to clean it up. I learned the taste. I learned the weight of the smoke. I learned the feeling of making something.
